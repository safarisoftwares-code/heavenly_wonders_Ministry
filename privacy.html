Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Heavenly Wonders Ministry respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what we do and do not do with your information.

1. What we collect

We do not track you, profile you, or sell your data. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics tools. If you email us or contact us via WhatsApp, we receive the information you choose to send (such as your name, email address, or message). We use that information only to reply to you.

2. Cookies

Our website uses one small cookie-like preference: a browser storage entry that remembers whether you chose Light Mode or Dark Mode. This never leaves your device.

3. External links

Our site links to Bible study resources on GitHub Pages, to WhatsApp, and to downloadable PDFs. When you click those links, those platforms have their own privacy policies. We do not control them.

4. Children

Our resources are suitable for all ages, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page. Any change will be visible here.

6. Contact

If you have any questions, you can reach us at biblestudyprograme@gmail.com.
